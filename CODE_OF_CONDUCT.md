# Code of conduct

Playroom is a small project made by people who like games and homelabs. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background, identity or favourite console.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second language.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- Accept that the maintainers decide what goes into the project, and explain your disagreement calmly if you have one.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or discriminatory jokes and remarks.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, including their accounts, keys or gaming profiles, without their permission.
- Spam and self-promotion unrelated to the discussion.
- Requests for pirated games or ROMs.

## Where it applies

In the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and in any space that represents the project, such as the HexGuard Discord server when people talk about Playroom there.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately through the [HexGuardSec GitHub profile](https://github.com/HexGuardSec), or a moderator on the Discord server. Reports are kept confidential.

## Consequences

Depending on how serious it is: a reminder, a removed message, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces. The maintainers decide, and will explain their decision to the person concerned.
